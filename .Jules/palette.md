## YYYY-MM-DD - Context-Aware Alt Text
**Learning:** Alt text for images in narrative structures (like the Markdown chapters of a book) needs to be heavily contextualized. Purely visual descriptions might spoil a narrative puzzle or be redundant if the text immediately explains the image.
**Action:** Always read the paragraphs directly before and after an image to extract exactly the rhetorical purpose and use that literal text as the alt text.
