## 2024-06-04 - Image Alt Text Accessibility in Markdown
**Learning:** Images embedded in Markdown files often lack alt text, creating a poor experience for screen reader users who miss critical visual context. Furthermore, alt text should not simply duplicate surrounding body text; it must uniquely convey the image's rhetorical or narrative purpose, especially in context-heavy documents.
**Action:** Always verify that important informational or narrative images have concise, context-aware alt text that aids accessibility without redundancy.
