## 2024-05-15 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Narrative Documents
**Learning:** In narrative-driven Markdown documents, purely visual alt text for images (e.g. "a cat with sewn eyes") fails to convey the author's intended message. Accessibility relies on understanding *why* the image is present, not just *what* it is.
**Action:** Always derive image alt text from the surrounding argumentative or narrative context rather than providing literal visual descriptions, ensuring screen reader users grasp the conceptual point being illustrated.
