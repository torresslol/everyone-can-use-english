## 2024-06-25 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Images

**Learning:** When adding alt text to images in a book or narrative context, the alt text shouldn't just describe the image visually. It must be derived from the surrounding rhetorical context to be useful for screen reader users, capturing the arguments or narrative points the author is making. Simple verbatim repetition of the preceding text is also unhelpful and redundant.
**Action:** Always extract and synthesize the surrounding narrative context when writing alt text for manuscript images, ensuring it serves the rhetorical purpose of the text without unnecessary redundancy.
