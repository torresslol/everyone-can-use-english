## 2024-05-20 - Contextual Alt Text in Narrative Markdown
**Learning:** In purely narrative formats, image alt text must rely on surrounding rhetorical arguments (e.g., conceptual descriptions like 'Localizationism considers the brain a complex machine') rather than redundant visual descriptions.
**Action:** Extract adjacent conceptual explanations to serve as alt text instead of guessing visual contents, preventing spoilers and repetition for screen reader users.
