## 2026-03-23 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Rhetorical Images
**Learning:** In narrative-heavy content like 'Everyone Can Use English', images often serve a specific rhetorical purpose (e.g., demonstrating a psychological concept like pattern recognition) rather than purely visual illustration. Traditional descriptive alt text fails to convey the point the author is making.
**Action:** Always derive image alt text from the surrounding narrative context to explain *why* the image is there, not just *what* it is, ensuring screen reader users understand the argument being made.
