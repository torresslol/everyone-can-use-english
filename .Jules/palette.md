
## 2026-04-24 - Narrative-driven Alt Text for Manuscript Images
**Learning:** Images in this Markdown manuscript often serve as rhetorical devices (e.g., demonstrating brain plasticity) rather than mere illustrations. Standard visual alt text fails to convey their purpose to screen reader users.
**Action:** When adding alt text to manuscript images, always read surrounding paragraphs to synthesize the rhetorical argument into the alt text.
