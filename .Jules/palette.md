## 2024-05-20 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Narrative Flow
**Learning:** When adding alt text to images preceding questions or reveals in narrative text, providing visual-only alt text without context or revealing answers prematurely can spoil the narrative flow for screen reader users.
**Action:** Synthesize surrounding text to provide context-aware alt text, like setting up a rhetorical question (e.g., using "配合下文提问引出关于...") to preserve the intended flow.
