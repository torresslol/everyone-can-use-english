## 2024-03-13 - Contextual Alt Text in Rhetorical Documents
**Learning:** When adding alt text to images in a book or narrative document, purely visual descriptions (e.g., "a cat") are insufficient and fail to convey the author's rhetorical purpose. The alt text must capture the context-aware argument the image supports.
**Action:** Always read the surrounding paragraphs to derive the true narrative purpose of an image before assigning alt text.
