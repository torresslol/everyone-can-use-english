## 2024-05-20 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Rhetorical Images
**Learning:** In narrative or argumentative Markdown texts, pure visual descriptions for images are insufficient. The alt text must synthesize the surrounding text's argument (e.g., explaining "Localizationism" or "Critical Period") so screen reader users understand the rhetorical purpose of the illustration.
**Action:** Always extract preceding and succeeding paragraphs to determine the rhetorical context of an image before writing its alt text.
