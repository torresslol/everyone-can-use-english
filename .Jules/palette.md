## 2023-11-20 - Narrative Image Alt Text Constraints
**Learning:** When adding alt text to images embedded in a rhetorical narrative, deriving the alt text from the surrounding explicit narrative context is required. However, care must be taken to not pre-emptively reveal or "spoil" answers to rhetorical questions or guessing games posed in the preceding text, to ensure screen reader users have the same rhetorical experience.
**Action:** Extract exact rhetorical context from surrounding sentences for alt text, but carefully filter to omit 'spoilers' to immediate questions before the image.
