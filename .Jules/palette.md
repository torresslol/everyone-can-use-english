## 2026-06-28 - Image Alt Text Accessibility in Pedagogical Content
**Learning:** When adding alt text to images used for interactive, pedagogical questions (e.g., visual quizzes), the alt text must describe the visual state accurately without prematurely spoiling the answers to the questions in the subsequent text.
**Action:** Ensure alt text strictly describes the image contents rather than copying pedagogical questions or answers from surrounding body text, avoiding redundant recitals for screen reader users.
