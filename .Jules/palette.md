## 2026-05-29 - Contextual Alt Text for Images

**Learning:** Alt text for images should be context-aware and derive from the surrounding narrative arguments (the rhetorical purpose), rather than being purely visual descriptions. This avoids redundancy and provides a better experience for screen reader users.

**Action:** When crafting alt text, always read the text preceding and following the image to understand its context. Avoid preemptively revealing information that spoils interactive elements or questions posed to the reader. Ensure the alt text accurately reflects the image's role within the specific narrative context.
