## 2024-05-24 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Narrative Images
**Learning:** In narrative-driven Markdown repositories, purely visual descriptions for images are insufficient for accessibility. Alt text must capture the specific rhetorical purpose of the image derived from the surrounding text to convey the author's point to screen reader users.
**Action:** Always derive Markdown image alt text from the surrounding contextual arguments rather than just describing the image visually.
