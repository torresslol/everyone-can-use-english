## 2024-05-18 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Narrative Markdown
**Learning:** Purely visual alt texts in narrative-heavy content fail to convey the author’s argument. In this project, images serve as evidence for concepts (like brain plasticity or CI therapy) rather than just decorative illustrations.
**Action:** When adding alt text to narrative Markdown, ensure the description reflects the surrounding argument and educational purpose of the image, rather than just describing its visual contents.
