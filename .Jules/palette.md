## 2024-05-24 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Chinese

**Learning:** This repository contains a Chinese manuscript ("Everyone Can Use English"). Image alt texts must be context-aware and derived from the surrounding narrative arguments (the rhetorical purpose), rather than being purely visual descriptions. Furthermore, they must be written in Chinese to match the primary language of the text.

**Action:** When adding alt texts to images in this repository, always read the surrounding Chinese text to understand the narrative context, and formulate the alt text in Chinese to describe the image's purpose within that argument.
