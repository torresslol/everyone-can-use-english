## 2024-05-24 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Markdown Images
**Learning:** When adding alt text to images in a purely Markdown-based content repository, screen readers benefit far more from synthesizing the narrative and rhetorical context of the surrounding text than from a purely visual description or verbatim repetition of the preceding paragraph.
**Action:** Always derive image alt text from the surrounding argument or narrative context rather than the visual appearance of the image alone.
