## 2026-03-26 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Narrative Markdown
**Learning:** When adding accessibility alt text to images in a book-like narrative structure, the description must be derived from the surrounding rhetorical arguments rather than just visual contents. For example, a picture of a book cover isn't just "a book cover", it's "Kirkham's Grammar (1826) demonstrating Lincoln's dedication to learning".
**Action:** Always extract at least a paragraph of surrounding context to understand the author's rhetorical purpose before drafting alt text for narrative images.
