## 2026-03-17 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Markdown Narratives
**Learning:** For a manuscript heavy in rhetoric and argumentation, visual alt texts should not merely describe the visual content (e.g., "a boy's eyes"). They must convey the underlying argumentative purpose (e.g., "海洋吉普赛人男孩的眼睛展示了通过训练控制瞳孔收缩的大脑可塑性" - A sea gypsy boy's eyes demonstrating brain plasticity through training).
**Action:** Always derive image alt texts from the surrounding text's narrative argument rather than the standalone visual content, especially in long-form Chinese Markdown chapters.
