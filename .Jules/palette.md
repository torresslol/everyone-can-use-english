## 2024-05-24 - Narrative Context for Markdown Alt Text
**Learning:** When authoring markdown that is prose-heavy (like a book or manuscript), image alternative text must be context-aware and serve the surrounding argument. Visual-only descriptions fail to capture the purpose of the image in the text.
**Action:** When adding alt text to narrative content, derive the description from the surrounding paragraphs and arguments to ensure screen reader users get the full meaning of why the image is included.
