## 2026-06-13 - Avoid Spoilers in Alt Text
**Learning:** When crafting alt text for images within a narrative, avoid preemptively revealing information that spoils interactive 'guessing games' or questions posed to the reader in the surrounding text, as this degrades the experience for screen reader users.
**Action:** Derive alt text from contextual descriptions without duplicating spoiler questions or answering them prematurely.
