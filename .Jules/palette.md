## 2024-05-15 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Markdown Manuscripts
**Learning:** In purely rhetorical markdown manuscripts (like 'Everyone Can Use English'), images often serve as illustrative evidence rather than standalone visuals. Standard descriptive alt text fails a11y needs; alt text must be derived directly from the surrounding narrative arguments to provide equivalent meaning for screen reader users.
**Action:** When adding alt text to narrative documents, read the surrounding paragraphs first and craft descriptions that explicitly connect the visual to the author's rhetorical point.
