## 2026-03-18 - Context-aware alt text for narrative imagery
**Learning:** Alt text in long-form narrative content (like chapters in a book) should capture the author's rhetorical intent rather than just visually describing the image. Visual descriptions alone fail to connect the image to the surrounding narrative context.
**Action:** When adding alt text to narrative or educational content, derive the description from the surrounding arguments and context to ensure screen reader users grasp the intended rhetorical point of the image.
