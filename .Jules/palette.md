## 2024-05-22 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Chapter 1
**Learning:** In narrative-driven texts like "Everyone Can Use English", images serve specific rhetorical purposes (e.g., proving brain plasticity) rather than just being visual decorations. Standard descriptive alt text (like "a boy in water") fails to convey the author's argument.
**Action:** Always derive alt text from the surrounding narrative context to ensure screen reader users can follow the logical flow of the argument, not just receive visual descriptions.
