## 2024-05-24 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Educational Markdown
**Learning:** In narrative/educational content, image alt text should convey the rhetorical argument or evidence the image represents (e.g., 'proving brain plasticity'), rather than just its visual contents. This drastically improves screen-reader accessibility for complex concepts.
**Action:** When adding alt text to educational materials, derive the description from the surrounding narrative arguments rather than purely visual attributes.
