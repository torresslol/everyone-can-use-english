## 2024-05-24 - Narrative Alt Text
**Learning:** In purely text-heavy markdown like this book, alt text derived solely from visual contents is often redundant or missing the point. The alt text needs to capture the rhetorical purpose of the image within the surrounding narrative argument.
**Action:** When adding alt texts to markdown books/articles, extract surrounding paragraphs to determine the image's role as evidence or illustration, and write alt text that synthesizes this rhetorical context.
