## 2026-06-10 - Image Alt Text in Narratives
**Learning:** When crafting alt text for images in a narrative document, do not simply copy the adjacent text verbatim or preemptively reveal information that spoils questions posed to the reader (e.g., 'guess which arm is injured'). Doing so degrades the experience for screen reader users by forcing them to hear redundant sentences or spoiling the intended flow.
**Action:** Always read both preceding and following paragraphs for context and craft concise descriptions that respect the narrative structure and avoid redundancy.
