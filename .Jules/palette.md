## 2026-05-02 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Narrative Content
**Learning:** In narrative-driven Markdown content, image alt text must avoid preemptively revealing answers to rhetorical questions or "guessing games" posed to the reader in the surrounding text. Pre-spoiling visual puzzles severely degrades the experience for screen reader users by removing the interactive element.
**Action:** Always scan the surrounding text (both preceding and following) for questions directed at the reader before authoring alt text, and keep descriptions neutral to preserve the narrative flow.
