## 2024-03-27 - Context-Aware Alt Text for Markdown Images
**Learning:** Image alt text in the Markdown chapters must be context-aware and derived from the surrounding narrative arguments (the rhetorical purpose) rather than purely visual descriptions. This significantly enhances accessibility for users relying on screen readers.
**Action:** Always derive alt text by reading the preceding and succeeding paragraphs to understand why the image is included in the narrative. Write the alt text in the primary language of the document (Chinese).
