## 2024-05-20 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Narrative Markdown
**Learning:** In a heavily narrative Markdown structure, images often serve as rhetorical devices (e.g., demonstrating brain plasticity or critical periods) rather than purely visual data. Standard descriptive alt text fails screen reader users by missing the argumentative purpose.
**Action:** Synthesize the surrounding narrative arguments into the alt text rather than repeating the text verbatim or merely describing the visual.
