## 2024-05-25 - Context-Aware Image Alt Text
**Learning:** For accessibility in this Markdown-based manuscript, images should not just describe the literal visual (which may not load), but instead use surrounding narrative context (like identifying specific people, places, or rhetorical arguments, such as "David Hubel and Torsten Wiesel" or "Lascaux cave paintings") as alt text.
**Action:** Always extract text from the paragraphs immediately surrounding an image to form precise, meaningful alt text instead of guessing visual contents.
