## 2026-03-22 - Context-Aware Alt Text in Narrative Content
**Learning:** In narrative manuscripts, images are often not merely decorative or literal representations, but rhetorical devices used to make specific arguments (e.g., using an optical illusion to prove that "feelings are unreliable"). Purely visual descriptions fail to convey the author's intent to screen-reader users.
**Action:** Always extract and analyze the surrounding narrative text to derive the rhetorical purpose of an image before writing its alt text, rather than relying solely on describing its visual appearance.
