## 2024-05-15 - Alt text from narrative arguments
**Learning:** Alt text for images in narrative structures should be context-aware and derived from the surrounding arguments (the rhetorical purpose), rather than being purely visual descriptions, especially to avoid revealing interactive 'guessing games' or questions.
**Action:** Always read the text preceding and following the image to determine its conceptual purpose before adding alt text, rather than just describing the image visually.
