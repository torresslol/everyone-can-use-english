## 2024-05-24 - Context-aware Alt Text for Markdown Images
**Learning:** In purely markdown content, alt text for images must be derived directly from the surrounding narrative rather than purely visual descriptions to ensure a seamless experience for screen reader users and to avoid redundant reading of the surrounding text.
**Action:** When adding alt text, always analyze the preceding and following paragraphs to synthesize an informative, contextually accurate description without verbatim repetition.
