
## 2026-06-12 - Contextual Alt Text
**Learning:** In purely markdown-based narratives, alt text for images cannot be visually derived if the image is missing or cannot be viewed. The surrounding text context often provides explicitly correct factual details that make for highly grounded and accurate alt text without hallucination.
**Action:** When adding alt text to images in markdown books or articles, meticulously read both preceding and following paragraphs to extract factual descriptors or the rhetorical purpose of the image.
